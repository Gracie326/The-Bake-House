WHAT GOES INTO OUR MUFFINS

Simple ingredients, lovely flavours.

Every ingredient brings something special to the mix.

01

Banana

Bananas bring natural sweetness, flavour, and moisture to our muffins. They also provide potassium and fibre.

02

Oats

Oats add texture and provide fibre, including beta-glucan, which supports healthy digestion and heart health.

03

Chocolate

Chocolate brings that rich, familiar flavour that makes every bite feel like a treat. Its nutritional value depends on the type used.
